Targeter is a set of apps that share one login. One account and password works in every app, and access to each app is granted per user with a role.

## Who does what

| Level | Manages |
|---|---|
| Partner | A group of companies |
| Company | Offices, branding, API access |
| Office | Users, connected ad accounts, campaigns |
| User | Creates and follows campaigns |

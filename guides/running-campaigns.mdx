## Create a campaign

Choose **New campaign** and decide what to do: **Paid advertising** or **Organic posts**.

1. Pick an active [campaign plan](/guides/campaign-plans). If none are listed, an admin must create one first.
2. Add the name, start and end dates, total budget, and the link the ads should lead to.
3. Add images and videos, and optionally text such as selling points or viewing times.
4. For each ad in the plan, choose how it is made: **Generate new**, **Use from the Editor**, or **Use the images** as they are.
5. Fill in any template fields Targeter could not find in the material.
6. Optionally let Targeter create a shareable [report](/guides/reports) automatically.

Targeter then analyses the material, suggests a strategy and creates ad drafts. The campaign page updates when it is ready.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Created, not approved |
| Setting up | Targeter is building the ad drafts |
| Error | Setup failed, contact an administrator |
| Missing content | One or more ads are missing images |
| Ready | Drafts are ready for review |
| Scheduled | Approved, waiting for the start date |
| Active | Running |
| Paused | Stopped and can be resumed |
| Completed | Ended |

## Review and approve

Open the campaign and check each tab:

- **Ads**: each ad's platform strategy, copy variant, call to action, budget and images.
- **Texts**: the title and text pairs the ads use. Each copy needs a title and text.
- **Location**: search for places and set a radius. The minimum is 1 km for housing ads.
- **Budget**: see how the total is allocated across ads. An ad cannot be saved if the total would exceed the campaign budget, so increase the budget first.
- **Media**: upload and organise images, videos and HTML banners.

Then choose **Preview & approve**. Before a campaign can be approved:

- Every ad needs an ad type, a platform strategy and at least one image.
- At least one surface (Facebook or Instagram) must be selected.
- The office needs a default Facebook page.
- A web ad needs at least 3 headlines, 2 descriptions, 1 image or HTML banner, and a landing page URL. The office also needs a Google Ads Customer ID.

## During and after the campaign

- **Stop** pauses the campaign, and **Resume** or **Restart** starts it again.
- **Change dates** is only available while the campaign is paused. If the listing has a showing that does not match the end date, Targeter offers to adjust the dates.
- Changes to texts on a running campaign are sent to the platforms when you save.
- **Delete campaign** cannot be undone.

## Organic posts

Organic campaigns have no ad budget. Create posts for a Facebook page or Instagram account with text, media, an optional link and an optional scheduled time. You can publish now, edit or delete a post, and see impressions, reach and engagement once it is published.

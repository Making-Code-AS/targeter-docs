<Note>
Billing is in the Targeter Admin area and is only available to partners and higher roles.
</Note>

The billing overview lists finished campaigns for all active companies and offices, with their active period, office, report and billing status. Filter by date range and download an overview as PDF.

## Billing status

Each campaign has one status:

1. **Unbilled**
2. **Invoice created**
3. **Customer invoiced**
4. **Paid**

## Create an invoice

1. Choose the company.
2. Choose the offices.
3. Choose the campaigns to include.
4. Set the cost for every campaign.
5. Review the overview and save.

After saving you can download the invoice as PDF. Earlier invoices are listed under **Invoices**.

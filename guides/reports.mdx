---
title: 'Reports'
description: 'Create and share campaign reports'
---

Reports show the results of a campaign and update as data comes in. Data appears once the campaign starts delivering.

## Create a report

Choose **New report** and set:

- **Title** and an optional description.
- **Scope**: the whole campaign, or one or more ads.
- **Visible tabs**: choose at least one.
- **Publicly available**: allows anyone with the link to view the report.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Engagement & Reach | Impressions, reach and engagement over the period |
| Conversion & ROI | Clicks, conversions and cost per conversion |
| Budget & costs | Spend, CPC, CPM and budget allocation |
| Video | Viewer progress and which ads drive views |

## Share

Turn on **Publicly available** and copy the link. If it is turned off, the report page shows that it is not publicly available. Only people with permission can edit or delete a report.

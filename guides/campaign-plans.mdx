---
title: 'Campaign plans'
description: 'Budget tiers and the plans brokers choose from when creating a campaign'
---

Campaign plans are managed under **Admin → Campaign plans** in Targeter.

## 1. Create budget tiers

Budget tiers are amounts in NOK. Create at least one before creating plans. Tiers are sorted by amount, and a tier that is used by a plan cannot be deleted.

## 2. Create a campaign plan

A plan has:

- **Name and description**: what brokers see when they create a campaign.
- **Budget tier** and **geolocation**: currently taken from the office address.
- **Ads**: which ads are created when a campaign is set up. For each ad, choose the ad type, an editor template, its formats and fields.
- **Budget shares**: shares across the plan's ads must total 100.
- **Active**: only active plans can be picked.

A plan that is in use by campaigns cannot be deleted.

## 3. Brokers create campaigns

When a broker creates a campaign, they pick one of the active plans. A campaign moves through these statuses: draft, setup, ready, scheduled, active, paused and completed.

Organisation setup is done in the Targeter Admin dashboard.

## Structure

Partners contain companies, companies contain offices, and offices contain users.

## Create a company

Give the company a name and domain, and upload its logo and dark-mode logo. Targeter also makes PNG copies of the logos, because Gmail and Outlook do not show SVG in emails. If a conversion fails, the company is still saved and you see a warning.

## Create an office

Each office can optionally have:

- **Allowed Facebook pages**: limits which pages its users can select. An empty list means all pages are available.
- **Allowed Instagram accounts**: the same, for Instagram.
- **Google Ads Customer ID**: paste the 10-digit ID of the office's Google Ads sub-account, or create a new account from the form.

## Add users and set access

Users get an admin level and, per app, a role.

| Admin level | Scope | Can create |
|---|---|---|
| Office admin | Own office | Users |
| Company admin | Whole company | Users, offices |
| Partner admin | Whole partner | Users, offices, companies |
| Super admin | Everything | Users, offices, companies, partners, partner admins |

- You only see and manage users in your scope with a lower admin level than your own, never yourself.
- You can grant app access only to apps you have access to yourself, and only with roles at or below your own.

## Deleting

Offices, companies and partners can only be deleted when nothing references them. Deleting a company can also delete its campaigns, so check what depends on it first.
